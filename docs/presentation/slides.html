01 · The problem المشكلة

Three habits, three adult apps.

Scattered

A fitness tracker for the gym, a language app for words, a quiz app for trivia. Effort split three ways never becomes one habit.

Made for adults

Most apps are full of ads or paywalls, and are overwhelming for a 12-year-old.

No plan

Following YouTube workout videos has no levels, no structure and no way to remember what you joined.

Who it's for لمن هذا التطبيق

Students first, and the adults around them.

01

Students aged 12–18

Short daily goals in sports, languages and general knowledge, all in one place.

02

Teachers

A free tool to recommend, and Easy / Medium / Hard levels for class-vs-class quizzes.

03

Parents and schools

Something free and safe to point students to, with no adult account needed.

04

Clubs and language centres

Exercise videos and leveled sentences to use in training sessions.

Our solution الحل

Pick it. Do it. Tick it.

1

Pick

A habit and a challenge: sports, a language, or the quiz. Then press “Start challenge”.

→
2

One short activity at your level: a workout video, a sentence read aloud, or 10 questions.

→
3

Tick it Done and watch the bar fill on “My challenges”. Come back tomorrow.

0classes: plain variables, ifs and loops
