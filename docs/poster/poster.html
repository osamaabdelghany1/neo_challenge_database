NEO CHALLENGE

One app, three daily habits.

Train your body, grow your languages and sharpen your mind: pick a challenge, do one short activity, and come back tomorrow.

01 · WHYThree habits, three adult apps.لماذا؟

The problem المشكلة

Too many apps, made for adults

Teenagers spread their effort across a fitness tracker, a language app and a quiz app. Most are built for adults, full of ads or paywalls, and none helps a young person keep a small daily habit going.

Our solution الحل

One free place for all three

NEO Challenge puts sports challenges, language activities and a general knowledge quiz in one free web app for students aged 12–18. Join a challenge, tick off short activities, and watch the progress bar fill.

02 · SEE ITPick it. Do it. Tick it.اختر، نفّذ، أنجز

The French Conversation Challenge: joined, 9 of 30 sentences done, and a player under each sentence that reads it aloud.

  • Three habits, one home12 challenges: 8 Sports, 3 Language and 1 General Knowledge.
  • Join and tick“Start challenge”, then tick each activity Done. A progress bar shows how far you are.
  • Hear every sentenceEnglish, French and German: 30 sentences each at Easy, Medium and Hard, with 90 audio clips.
  • A quiz, one question at a time10 random questions per level, right or wrong straight away, and your score is saved.
  • 60 exercise videosCheck your fitness level, filter by equipment, level and muscle, then log “I did this today”.
  • Muscles and meals15 muscles with anatomy facts, and your BMI with a 7-day meal plan.

Home: the three habits, one button each.

Quiz: right or wrong straight away.

My challenges: progress, workouts and best scores.
